# Green Fibre Brand Story & Sustainability Mission

## Our Origins
Green Fibre was founded in 2021 by textile designers Elena Vance and Marcus Chen in Portland, Oregon. Frustrated by the rampant plastic pollution and synthetic microplastics shed by fast fashion, they set out to build an e-commerce brand centered entirely on pure, regenerative, and biodegradable plant fibers.

## Natural Materials We Use
Every garment and home textile at Green Fibre is crafted exclusively from sustainably sourced natural fibers:
- **GOTS-Certified Organic Cotton**: Cultivated without toxic pesticides, synthetic fertilizers, or genetically modified seeds. Uses 91% less water than conventional cotton through rainwater harvesting.
- **European Flax Linen**: Grown naturally in Normandy and Belgium. Flax requires zero irrigation beyond natural rainfall and zero chemical pesticides.
- **Himalayan Hemp**: Strong, naturally pest-resistant, and carbon-negative. Hemp replenishes nutrients in the soil through phytoremediation.
- **Closed-Loop Bamboo Lyocell**: Sourced from certified FSC organic bamboo forests and processed in a closed-loop system where 99% of non-toxic solvents and water are recycled.

## Our Core Commitments
- **Zero Synthetic Microplastics**: We never blend polyester, nylon, acrylic, or elastane into our core fabrics. All threads, buttons, and trims use natural alternatives like corozo nut buttons, organic cotton threads, and natural rubber elastic.
- **Circular Packaging**: Every customer shipment is packed in 100% post-consumer recycled boxes sealed with water-activated paper tape. Individual garment protective bags are made from marine-biodegradable cassava root starch that dissolves in hot water.
- **Wildflower Seed Hangtags**: Every price tag is embedded with non-GMO wildflower and thyme seeds. Customers can plant the tag in soil to grow garden blooms.
- **Fair Labor & Living Wages**: We partner exclusively with certified B-Corp mills in northern Portugal and fair-trade artisan cooperatives in Tamil Nadu, India. All workers earn living wages, receive healthcare coverage, and work in safe, dignified conditions.
