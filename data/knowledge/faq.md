# Greenie Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Product Material & Safety
### What is Greenie's upcycled rice-husk biocomposite?
Our products are handcrafted from agricultural rice husks—the natural byproduct left behind after harvesting rice. Instead of burning this biomass, we upcycle the natural silica and plant fibers into a durable, food-grade biocomposite with a smooth, ceramic-like feel.

### Are Greenie products plastic-free and food-safe?
Greenie products are crafted from upcycled agricultural rice husk biocomposite to minimize single-use plastics. Formal food-contact certifications, material testing, and migration compliance reports are documented on a per-SKU basis. Please check the individual product packaging or consult a store associate for SKU-specific test reports.

### Are Greenie products breakable like ceramic or glass?
Unlike fragile ceramic or glass, Greenie biocomposite drinkware and dining products are designed for everyday durability and shatter resistance.

## Care & Usage Instructions
### Can I put Greenie products in the microwave?
Microwave suitability varies by product design and material construction. For example, drinkware with stainless steel components or thermal insulation must never be placed in a microwave. Please check the bottom stamp and care instructions on the specific product package, or verify with a store associate.

### Are Greenie products dishwasher safe?
Dishwasher suitability depends on the specific item and cap/lid construction. While select solid biocomposite tableware items are top-rack dishwasher safe, products with silicone seals, wood-look finishes, or insulated liners should be hand-washed. Please check the individual product instructions or ask a store associate.

### How should I hand wash my Greenie drinkware?
Wash with warm water and mild liquid soap using a soft cloth or sponge. Avoid steel wool and abrasive scouring pads to maintain the smooth matte finish.
