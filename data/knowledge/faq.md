# Green Fibre Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Sustainable Garment Care
How should I wash Green Fibre clothing to maximize longevity?
- Wash in cold water (30 degrees Celsius or 86 degrees Fahrenheit) on a gentle cycle.
- Use plant-based, pH-neutral biodegradable laundry detergent.
- Avoid chlorine bleach, synthetic fabric softeners, and petroleum stain removers.
- Line dry in fresh air or tumble dry on delicate low heat. Natural drying prevents thermal fiber stress and conserves household energy.

## Sensitive Skin & Allergies
Are Green Fibre products safe for eczema and chemical sensitivities?
- Yes, completely. All our dyes are GOTS and OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified, free from toxic heavy metals, formaldehyde, and AZO compounds.
- Because our fabrics contain zero plastic synthetic microfibers, they are naturally hypoallergenic and highly breathable.
